Six thatched chalets above the Zambezi

A small, quiet lodge near Victoria Falls. Walk the bush at dawn, take a sundowner cruise on the river, and eat meals cooked over wood.

Message us on WhatsApp See rooms and rates

Stay

Each chalet has a centralised heating and ventilation system(HVAC), a private deck, a mosquito-netted bed, and a bathroom with hot water. Solar lighting and a backup battery pack keep the lights and phone charging on during power cuts.

Do

Guided walks, river boat cruises, sunset bird watchings and trips to the Falls can be arranged on the day or before you arrive. Tell us what you would like and we will plan the days around it.

Eat

Breakfast and dinner are included. We cook with local ingredients and can cater for vegetarian and other dietary needs if you tell us ahead of time.

How to book

  1. Message us your dates and the number of guests.
  2. We reply with availability and the price in US dollars.
  3. Pay a deposit to confirm your stay.

No forms and no account to create. Most people book from their phone in a few messages.
