Best time to visit

Dry season, April to August: cooler days with a slight chill in winter, and wildlife is easier to see around water due too high visibility.

Rainy season, September to March: green landscapes, many birds, and afternoon storms.

Message us if you are not sure which suits you.

Getting here

We are about 25 minutes by road from Victoria Falls Airport. We can collect you if you send your flight details. Driving yourself instead? Ask us for directions and we will send a pin on WhatsApp.

Before you travel

Check the current visa and entry rules for your passport before you book flights. Bring a hat, sunscreen, and a light jacket for early mornings.
